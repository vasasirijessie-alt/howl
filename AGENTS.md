# Notes for AI helpers

Howl is a simple social app for NC State students to share short posts about
campus life. Keep the real app plain unless asked to change its appearance.

Use the `explorations/` folder for separate, numbered design rounds (`01`,
`02`, `03`, and so on); do not edit the real app while trying ideas there.

The project owner is a beginner and does not use the terminal. Prefer simple
changes, explain any necessary steps in plain language, and do not assume they
can run terminal commands.
